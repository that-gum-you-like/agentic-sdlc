# How to Follow Along During Class

**Read this before the first workshop!**

## TL;DR

**You have two options:**
1. **Watch & Learn** - Focus on understanding, build your project after class with the recording
2. **Build Along** - Work on your project during designated "work time" blocks

**We recommend Option 1 for your first time through.** You can always rewatch and build later.

---

## The Class Format

This is a **hybrid teaching format**: part demonstration, part hands-on, part Q&A.

### How It Works

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ DEMONSTRATION (20-30 min)                               │
│ ↓ Teacher codes, you watch & take notes                │
│                                                          │
│ WORK TIME (10-15 min)                                   │
│ ↓ You try it on your project                           │
│ ↓ Breakout rooms available with TAs                    │
│                                                          │
│ DEMONSTRATION (20-30 min)                               │
│ ↓ Teacher codes, you watch & take notes                │
│                                                          │
│ WORK TIME (10-15 min)                                   │
│ ↓ You try it on your project                           │
│                                                          │
│ [Repeat throughout the day]                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Clear Signals

**When demonstration starts:**
> "Alright, I'm going to show you how to [do X]. Watch what I do, I'll explain as I go."

**When work time starts:**
> "Now it's your turn. Take 10 minutes to [do X on your project]. I'll start the breakout rooms if you need help."

**When to return from work time:**
> "Coming back in 2 minutes! Finish up what you're doing."

## Strategy 1: Watch & Learn (Recommended for First Time)

**During class:**
- ✅ Watch the demonstrations
- ✅ Take notes on what you want to try
- ✅ Ask questions in chat
- ✅ Use work time to review what was just covered (don't try to catch up)

**After class:**
- ✅ Rewatch the recording at your own pace
- ✅ Pause whenever you need to
- ✅ Build your project following along
- ✅ Use the syllabus as a checklist

**Pros:**
- You won't miss important concepts
- Less stressful
- Can rewatch at 1.5x speed for parts you understand
- Can pause and debug without falling behind

**Cons:**
- You won't have a working project by end of class
- Might feel less engaged

---

## Strategy 2: Build Along

**During demonstrations (20-30 min blocks):**
- ✅ Watch and take notes
- ❌ Don't try to code during demonstrations
- ❌ Don't try to troubleshoot during demonstrations

**During work time (10-15 min blocks):**
- ✅ Try what was just demonstrated on your project
- ✅ Join breakout room if you get stuck
- ❌ Don't try to catch up on previous exercises

**If you fall behind:**
- ✅ Take notes on what you missed
- ✅ Focus on the current demonstration
- ✅ Catch up during lunch or after class with the recording

**Pros:**
- You'll have a working project by end of class (if all goes well)
- More hands-on learning
- Immediate feedback

**Cons:**
- Easy to fall behind
- Might miss concepts while troubleshooting
- Stressful if things break

---

## What to Do When You Fall Behind

**DO NOT TRY TO CATCH UP DURING DEMONSTRATIONS.**

Instead:
1. Stop what you're doing
2. Watch the current demonstration
3. Take notes: "I need to go back and [do the thing I missed]"
4. During the next work time, decide:
   - Option A: Keep watching demonstrations, catch up after class
   - Option B: Use breakout room to catch up with TA help

**Remember:** The recording and syllabus are available. You can always finish later.

---

## Using Breakout Rooms

**When to use them:**
- ✅ During designated work time blocks
- ✅ When you're stuck on something specific
- ✅ When you need 1-on-1 help

**When NOT to use them:**
- ❌ During demonstrations (you'll miss new content)
- ❌ To ask general questions (use main room chat for those)

**How they work:**
- Teacher will say "Breakout rooms are open for the next 10 minutes"
- Click "Join Breakout Room" when ready
- TA will help debug your specific issue
- Return when teacher calls everyone back

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Mistake: "I'm trying to do everything at once"

**Fix:** Choose Watch & Learn OR Build Along. Don't try both simultaneously.

### ❌ Mistake: "I got stuck on setup and missed the first hour"

**Fix:** Setup should be done 2+ days before class. If you're stuck day-of, switch to Watch & Learn mode.

### ❌ Mistake: "I'm trying to debug while the teacher is explaining something new"

**Fix:** Stop debugging. Watch the demonstration. Debug during work time or after class.

### ❌ Mistake: "I don't know which terminal to use"

**Fix:**
- Mac: Terminal.app (the one that comes with Mac)
- Windows: PowerShell (search for "PowerShell" in start menu, NOT "Command Prompt")

### ❌ Mistake: "I'm using Cursor/Claude web instead of Claude Code"

**Fix:** This class uses Claude Code (the CLI tool). Install it from https://docs.anthropic.com/claude-code

## Accessibility & Learning Styles

### If you have ADHD or attention challenges:

**Recommendation:** Use Watch & Learn strategy.
- You can pause/rewind when rewatching
- Less pressure to multitask
- Clear focus: just watch during class

**Use work time to:**
- Review what was just covered
- Ask questions in chat
- Take structured notes

### If you're a visual learner:

- The syllabus has diagrams and code examples
- Teacher shares screen during all demonstrations
- You can screenshot important moments

### If you're a hands-on learner:

- Build Along strategy might work better for you
- But be strict about stopping during demonstrations
- Use office hours for extra hands-on time

### If you need accommodations:

- Ask in chat or email before class
- TAs can provide extra support in breakout rooms
- All materials available for review after class

## What to Do During Breaks

### ✅ Good uses of break time:
- Catch up on previous exercise
- Ask questions in chat
- Review notes
- Stretch, hydrate, bio break

### ❌ Don't use break time for:
- Starting the next exercise (you'll miss the demonstration)
- Deep debugging (you'll lose track of time)

## Questions During Class

### Via Chat:
- ✅ "What section are we in?"
- ✅ "Can you repeat that command?"
- ✅ "Where do I find [file/resource]?"
- ✅ Conceptual questions about what's being demonstrated

### Via Breakout Room:
- ✅ "My code isn't working, can you help debug?"
- ✅ "I'm stuck on Exercise X, can you walk me through it?"
- ✅ "How do I [specific technical thing]?"

### Save for After Class:
- ❌ "Can you review my entire project?"
- ❌ "How would I apply this to [complex custom scenario]?"
- ❌ Questions that need 10+ minutes to answer

## Final Tips

1. **Don't panic if you fall behind.** Everyone does. The recording is your friend.

2. **It's okay to just watch.** You're learning even if you're not coding along.

3. **Use the syllabus as your map.** It shows exactly where we're going.

4. **Ask "where are we?" anytime.** No question is too simple.

5. **Trust the process.** This format has worked for hundreds of students.

6. **You have the recording forever.** You can redo this class as many times as you want.

7. **The real learning happens when you build your own project after class.** Class is just the introduction.
